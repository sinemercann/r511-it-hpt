What Are IT and HPT?

Instructional Technology (IT)

IT helps us plan, use, and check teaching methods and materials. We decide what learners need to learn, choose methods and materials that fit the goal, and check what learners can do (Reiser, 2001a).

In class. A teacher uses fraction strips to show how to compare fractions. Students practice and get feedback. Then they explain which fraction is larger and why.

Human Performance Technology (HPT)

HPT helps people and teams do their work better. We compare what people do now with what we expect them to do, find the reasons for the difference, choose a solution, and check the results (Hampton Roads ISPI, n.d.).

In school. If teachers give feedback at different times, the school team can ask why. Teachers may need practice, more time, or a simple feedback form.

How are they different?

QuestionITHPT
Main goalHelp people learn.Help people and teams do their work better.
Starting questionWhat do learners need to learn?What do we expect people to do? What makes it hard?
Possible actionsPlan lessons, make materials, and give practice and feedback.Offer training, make guides, provide technical help, or change work routines.
What we checkCan learners use the new knowledge or skill?Are people doing what is expected more often or more effectively?

What do they have in common?

Both fields look at a need, set a goal, choose a solution, and check the results. HPT can help a school find out why teachers have trouble giving feedback. If teachers need practice writing feedback, IT can help the school plan the training. The team can then check what teachers learned and how they use it in class.
