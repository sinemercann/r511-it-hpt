My Perspective

When I worked as an educational technology specialist in an elementary school, I helped teachers use digital tools in their lessons. We first decided what students should learn. Then we chose a tool and planned how students would show what they learned. To me, Instructional Technology means making these decisions together so each part of the lesson helps students reach the same goal.

After we planned an activity, I also thought about whether teachers could use it in their classrooms. They needed enough time, access to devices, and help with technical problems. To me, Human Performance Technology means finding out what makes a task hard and choosing support that helps teachers do it.

What will students learn from this activity? What do teachers need to use it in their lessons?
